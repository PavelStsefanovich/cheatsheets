# Good Habits

Collection of good habits for working with agents.

## Habits To keep your AI desk clean and avoid hitting token limits

- Edit your mistakes: Use the edit button to fix typos or clarify your request instead of arguing with the AI in a new message.
- Ask related questions together: Combine all your questions from a single document set into one prompt and define your desired output format (e.g., bullet points or a one-pager) to reduce ambiguity.
- Start a clean task: When shifting to a new job, start a fresh conversation instead of letting old, irrelevant context carry forward and consume tokens.
- Carry the answer, not the argument: Between multi-step processes, only forward the resulting artifact or key information to the next stage rather than stacking the entire history of edits and rejected sources.
- Ask for exactly what you need: Specify the precise output format you require (e.g., a specific word count or JSON structure) to minimize redundant output tokens that would otherwise be sent back in future prompts.
- Search files yourself: Instead of forcing the model to read entire documents, perform the search yourself and provide only the relevant snippets or passages to the AI.
- Use the lightest source format: Convert files like PDFs into simple text or markdown before pasting them, as complex layouts unnecessarily consume token capacity.
- Keep your desk organized: (Implied through the theme) Maintain your sources and retrieved information in an organized manner.
- Use a retrieval database: Keep your accepted answers in a searchable database (like OpenBrain) so the AI can easily reference previous results instead of recalculating or searching for information from scratch.
